Pathology Synopsis

Water-soaked lesions appear on leaves, rapidly turning dark brown with white sporulation on the underside. Can destroy entire fields within 7–10 days under humid conditions.

Active Recovery Protocol

Recommended Plan: Tomato Late Blight

Apply targeted fungicide spray and prune infected lower canopy foliage immediately to arrest spore multiplication.

1
Prune & Dispose Infected Leaves

Remove leaves showing water-soaked lesions. Sterilize pruning shears in 70% isopropyl alcohol between plants to prevent mechanical spore transfer.

2
Bordeaux Mixture (1%) Spray

Dissolve 1kg Copper Sulphate + 1kg Quick Lime in 100L water. Spray thoroughly on both leaf surfaces in the late afternoon to avoid scorching.

3
Bio-Fungicide Trichoderma

Apply Trichoderma viride / harzianum at 5g/L around the root zone and canopy to competitively displace Phytophthora mycelia.

A
Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil Gold)

Dosage: 2.5g per Liter of water. Systemic + contact protection. Apply early morning with knapsack sprayer for complete leaf coverage.

B
Cymoxanil + Mancozeb (Curzate)

Dosage: 2g per Liter of water. Curative kick-back action within 48h of infection event. Maximum 2 consecutive applications.

C
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI)

Maintain a strict 7-day withholding period prior to harvesting fruit for human consumption to adhere to food safety MRL thresholds.
